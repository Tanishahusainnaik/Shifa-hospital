About Shifa Hospital

Serving Vijayapura and North Karnataka since 2001.

Our story

Shifa Hospital began as a 40-bed clinic and has grown into a 350-bed multispeciality hospital. Today more than 120 specialists treat patients across ten departments, with one shared record so your care team always sees the full picture.

Mission

Deliver safe, affordable and compassionate care to every patient.

Vision

To be the region's most trusted hospital for quality and kindness.
